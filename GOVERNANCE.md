# Project Governance

## 1. Overview

The **Khmer AI Language Reference** project is an open-source, community-governed effort dedicated to building high-quality, transparent Khmer linguistic resources for artificial intelligence.

Our governance model ensures linguistic neutrality, academic rigor, and continuous accessibility to the global developer and research communities.

---

## 2. Roles & Responsibilities

### Contributors
Anyone who opens issues, proposes new terminology, improves examples, or reports translation issues. All contributors follow [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

### Community Reviewers
Active contributors recognized for reliable, frequent peer reviews of proposed terminology and translations. They help review PRs and provide feedback on natural phrasing and contextual usage.

### Language Domain Experts
Native speakers, linguists, professional translators, or domain specialists (e.g. legal, healthcare, computer science) responsible for elevating entries from `community-reviewed` to `expert-reviewed` and `verified`.

### Maintainers / Steering Committee
Responsible for overall repository health, automated validation infrastructure, release tags, dispute resolution, and security oversight.

---

## 3. Decision-Making Process

1. **Consensus-Seeking**: Technical and linguistic choices are discussed publicly in GitHub Issues and Pull Requests.
2. **Authoritative Sources**: When debates occur regarding Khmer spelling or formal technical terms, preference is given to established publications from the **National Council of Khmer Language (ក្រុមប្រឹក្សាជាតិភាសាខ្មែរ - NCKL)** and recognized academic standards.
3. **Disambiguation Over Exclusion**: If two distinct translations are actively used in different contexts (e.g., formal legal vs. modern software UI), both are documented under distinct `context` or `alternativeKhmer` fields rather than forcing a single winner.
